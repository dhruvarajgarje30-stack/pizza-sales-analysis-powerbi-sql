# 🍕 Sales Performance & Revenue Analytics Dashboard (48,000+ Records)

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes over **48,000+ pizza sales records** to uncover key business insights related to revenue trends, product performance, and customer demand patterns.

The goal was to transform raw sales data into an **interactive Power BI dashboard** that helps stakeholders make data-driven decisions to improve revenue and operational efficiency.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

* **Power BI** – Data visualization & dashboard creation
* **SQL** – Data extraction, transformation, and analysis
* **Excel** – Data cleaning and preprocessing

---

## 📁 Dataset

* Contains **48,000+ transaction-level records**
* Includes:

  * Pizza ID
  * Order ID
  * Pizza name
  * Quantity
  * Order_Date & Order_Time
  * unit_price & total_price
  * Pizza Category & Size
  * pizza ingredients
  * Revenue

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights

* Identified **top-performing pizza categories** contributing highest revenue
* Discovered **peak order timings** and high-demand days
* Analyzed **monthly and daily revenue trends**
* Highlighted **low-performing products** affecting overall sales

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

* Revenue trend analysis (daily, monthly)
* Top & bottom performing products
* Sales distribution by category and size
* Interactive filters for dynamic exploration

---

## 💡 Business Impact

* Helps businesses **optimize product offerings**
* Supports **inventory planning based on demand trends**
* Enables **data-driven marketing strategies**
* Improves overall **revenue tracking and decision-making**
